Try the five steps

  1. Built on load. The task table was server-rendered; the Gantt replaced it with a task grid and a timeline.
  2. Swap. Pick the other project. hx-get fetches its tasks, the old Gantt is torn down and a new one is built.
  3. Out of band. Press "Server slips one task". One task comes back out of band and its bar moves a day.
  4. Edit. Drag a bar to new dates, or edit a cell. lattice:gantt-change is posted with hx-post and the server confirms below the chart.
  5. Back. Click Month in the timeline header, switch project, then press Back. The zoom returns to Month.
